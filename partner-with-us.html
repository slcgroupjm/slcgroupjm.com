Partnership

Meaningful work is stronger together.

Partnerships give organisations, businesses and community groups an opportunity to work alongside SLC Foundation in creating opportunities and supporting children and families.

Whether the focus is education, community outreach or corporate social responsibility, we welcome conversations about ways to collaborate around work that creates meaningful local impact.

A partnership can begin with one project and grow into something much more lasting.
Partnership Options

How we can work together.

Our partnership opportunities are built around three broad areas of collaboration.

01

Corporate Partnership

Support community initiatives through sponsorship, employee involvement, and social responsibility programmes.

02

Education Partnership

Help provide resources, learning opportunities, and support for children.

03

Community Partnership

Collaborate with us on outreach projects that create meaningful local impact.
