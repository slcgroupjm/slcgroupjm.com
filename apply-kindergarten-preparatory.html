Before You Apply

Registration Documents

Successful applicants will be required to complete registration in person and present the required documents to the school.

Please prepare:
  • Application Form (Pickup at the School)
  • Child's Birth Certificate — Original & Copy
  • Immunization Record Original & Copy — Front & Back, Pages 14 & 15
  • 2 Current Passport-sized Photographs
  • Recommendation, Last Report, and Financial Clearance from Current School
  • Parent / Guardian Identification
  • Student Registration Card — 6 years and above
