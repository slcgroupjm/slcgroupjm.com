Understanding Our Fees

Simple information for better planning.

We believe in making our fee structure clear and transparent for every family. Below you will find information about the main school fees, how payments may be made, and answers to frequently asked questions.

02 · Fee Breakdown

What families should know.

Each fee serves a different purpose and may follow a different payment schedule.

School Fee

Tuition

School or tuition fees are paid termly or yearly, at the beginning of each payment period.

Available payment methods include:

  • Online bank transfer (online website payments coming soon)
  • Debit/Credit Card using our Point of Sale (POS)
  • Bank Deposit
Meal Programme

Lunch Fee

Lunch fees are paid in full in cash at the beginning of each four-week period.

Annual Protection Package

M.I.P Protection Package

M.I.P stands for Maintenance, Insurance & PTA. This is a one-time annual fee paid at the beginning of each school year.

The package covers:

  • School maintenance
  • PTA contributions
  • Student accident insurance required by the Ministry of Education
Important Insurance coverage is required for enrolment.

Students cannot be enrolled without the insurance coverage included in the M.I.P Protection Package.

Payment Planning

Different payment methods may attract different banking, transaction, or service charges.
