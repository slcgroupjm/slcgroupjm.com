Sunshine Learning Center Group

A journey that grows with your child.

Sunshine Learning Center Group brings together Early Childhood education, Kindergarten & Preparatory learning, and community service through the SLC Foundation.

From a child's earliest discoveries through the primary years, our aim is to provide environments where learning, confidence, independence, character, and opportunity can grow together.

The Sunshine Experience

School should shape more than academics.

We believe children grow through what they learn, what they try, the relationships they build, the responsibilities they accept, and the experiences that help them discover who they can become.

01

Strong Foundations

Learning experiences designed to build knowledge, understanding, and the habits needed for continued growth.

02

Growing Independence

Helping children make decisions, accept responsibility, solve problems, and grow in confidence.

03

Life Beyond Lessons

Enrichment, activities, creativity, sport, performance, clubs, and opportunities to discover new interests.

04

Family & Community

Building partnerships among children, educators, families, and the communities in which learning happens.

Beyond the Classroom

Childhood is made of more than lessons.

Students are given opportunities to move, perform, create, collaborate, compete, communicate, and discover interests beyond the traditional classroom.

01

Music & Dance

Creative expression, performance, rhythm, movement, and confidence.

02

Swimming

Physical confidence, coordination, discipline, and water awareness.

03

Karate

Focus, discipline, physical development, confidence, and self-control.

04

Speech & Drama

05

06

Clubs & Enrichment

Spanish, Brownies, 4H, Spelling Bee, and other enrichment activities.
