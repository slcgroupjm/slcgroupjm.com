Christmas Raffle 2026: terms and conditions

Please read these before you buy a ticket.

Ticket pricesJ$1,000 adult, J$500 child
Submit tickets byThursday, 10 December 2026
Where to buyIn person only

  1. Raffle categories

    The Sunshine Learning Center Christmas Raffle consists of two separate draws: the Adult Christmas Raffle and the Children’s Christmas Raffle. Each raffle has its own ticket price, eligibility requirements and grand prize. Participants must make sure they buy tickets for the correct raffle category.

  2. Ticket prices and prizes

    Adult Christmas Raffle: tickets cost J$1,000 each. The grand prize is one air conditioning unit.

    Children’s Christmas Raffle: tickets cost J$500 each. The grand prize is one hoverboard.

  3. Ticket purchases

    All raffle tickets must be bought in person at any Sunshine Learning Center Early Childhood or Preparatory branch. Online raffle entries and online ticket purchases are not accepted. Participants must follow the ticket-purchasing instructions provided by the school and keep their ticket or proof of purchase for verification.

  4. Ticket submission deadline

    All raffle tickets must be submitted no later than Thursday, December 10, 2026. Tickets submitted after the deadline may be deemed ineligible for the draw. Participants are responsible for making sure their tickets are properly completed and submitted before the deadline.

  5. Eligibility for the adult raffle

    The Adult Christmas Raffle is intended for eligible adult participants. By buying a ticket, the participant confirms that they meet the applicable eligibility requirements. The organizer reserves the right to verify a winner’s eligibility before awarding the prize.

  6. Eligibility for the children’s raffle

    The Children’s Christmas Raffle is intended for children. A parent or legal guardian must buy the ticket on the child’s behalf and oversee any arrangements relating to the child’s participation and collection of a prize. The child’s name and any other required information must be provided accurately, in accordance with the school’s instructions.

  7. Draw procedure and winner selection

    Winners will be selected through the draw process communicated by Sunshine Learning Center. Each valid ticket represents one entry into the corresponding raffle, subject to the official ticket rules. The adult and children’s raffles are treated as separate draws. The organizer will take reasonable steps to make sure the draw is conducted fairly and that the winning ticket can be verified.

  8. Notification of winners

    Winners will be contacted using the contact information provided when the ticket was bought or registered. Participants are responsible for providing accurate contact details and remaining reachable after the draw. For the children’s raffle, the parent or legal guardian will be contacted about the winning child and prize-collection arrangements. The organizer will communicate any deadline for claiming a prize.

  9. Prize collection

    Winners must follow the instructions provided by Sunshine Learning Center to verify their winning ticket and collect their prize. Proof of the winning ticket and appropriate identification or other verification may be required. For a child winner, the parent or legal guardian must oversee the prize-collection process. Prizes are not transferable or redeemable for cash unless expressly stated in writing by the organizer.

  10. Invalid, lost or unverifiable tickets

    Tickets that are incomplete, illegible, altered, duplicated or otherwise unable to be verified may be disqualified, subject to the official raffle rules and applicable law. Participants should keep their tickets safe, as the organizer may be unable to replace lost or damaged tickets without sufficient proof of purchase.

  11. Changes or cancellation

    Sunshine Learning Center reserves the right to make reasonable changes to the raffle arrangements or cancel a draw where necessary, subject to applicable Jamaican law and any required authorization. Any significant changes will be communicated through the school’s official communication channels. The organizer will handle any refunds or other obligations in accordance with the applicable rules and legal requirements.

  12. Personal information

    Information collected for ticket sales, winner verification and prize collection will be used to run the raffle and for related communication, subject to applicable privacy and data-protection requirements. Children’s personal information should be provided by a parent or legal guardian where required.

  13. Acceptance of terms

    Buying or submitting a raffle ticket means you acknowledge these Terms and Conditions and the applicable official raffle rules. Participants and, where relevant, parents or legal guardians are responsible for reviewing the rules before taking part.
