Who We Are

Extending Sunshine beyond our school gates.

The SLC Foundation is the charitable arm of the SLC Group, dedicated to empowering children and families through education, outreach programmes, scholarships and community development.

Through partnerships, giving and practical support, the Foundation works to help create opportunities for children and families who may otherwise face barriers to the resources they need.

Our goal is simple: create brighter futures by providing resources, opportunities and support where it is needed most.
Our Purpose

How we serve.

Our work is rooted in three areas that guide the way we support children, families and communities.

01

Education

Providing resources, learning opportunities and support designed to help children succeed and strengthen their educational journey.

02

Community

Working alongside communities and partner organisations to create meaningful initiatives that respond to real needs.

03

Compassion

Supporting children and families with dignity, care and practical assistance when that support matters most.
