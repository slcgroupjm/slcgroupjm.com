01
Admissions Information

A welcoming admissions process for every family.

Sunshine Learning Center is proud of the diverse and inclusive learning environment we provide. We are committed to ensuring that every child is welcomed, supported, and encouraged to grow, learn, and develop to their fullest potential, regardless of background or ability.

Sunshine Learning Center operates under the SLC Group of schools and follows an admissions approach that promotes fairness and equal opportunity for families. Our goal is to provide children with access to safe, nurturing, and stimulating environments where learning can flourish.

Flexible payment plans are also available to support families while helping make quality education more accessible.

Prospective parents applying to either the Early Childhood or Kindergarten & Preparatory division may contact the respective school by email or phone, request a call, or begin through our online Apply Portal.

03 · The Process

I'm ready to apply. What happens next?

The admissions journey can be understood in four simple stages.

STEP 01

Complete the Application

Complete the appropriate application form and pay the applicable registration fee.

STEP 02

Student Assessment & Profile

Complete any required student assessment and provide information needed for the student's profile.

STEP 03

Orientation & Second Package

Complete and submit the second registration package at the appropriate orientation session.

STEP 04

Settle Fees & Prepare for School

Complete outstanding fees and dues, finalise registration, and prepare for the start of school.

Orientation Day

Your introduction to the Sunshine Learning Center community.

Registered parents are invited to attend the relevant orientation session. We believe that a strong partnership between home and school is an important part of a child's success, and orientation helps establish that relationship from the beginning.

During orientation, families will have an opportunity to connect with staff, explore our core programmes, tour the learning environment, and receive important information ahead of the school year.

04 · Additional Admissions Information

Applications outside the usual process.

Waiting Lists & Off-Season Registration

If you wish to apply after registration has closed or before registration has opened for the next academic year, please contact the relevant Early Childhood or Preparatory branch and schedule a meeting with the Director or Principal.

If available places have already been filled, families may request to have their child's name added to the waiting list. The waiting list is maintained on a first-come, first-served basis according to the date the application was received.

When a place becomes available, it is offered to the next child on the waiting list. If accepted, the remaining applicants move forward accordingly.

In special circumstances, the school reserves the right to prioritise a child where necessary, including matters relating to welfare or other exceptional considerations.
