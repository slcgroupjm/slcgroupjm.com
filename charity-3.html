Our Mission

Compassion in times of hardship.

At SLC Foundation, our mission is to serve communities through compassion, partnership, and meaningful action.

We are committed to supporting individuals and families during times of hardship by bringing people together to provide hope, encouragement, and essential assistance.

Through community-driven initiatives, we strive to make a lasting difference and remind those in need that they are supported.

The Response

Community coming together when it mattered.

Following the impact of Hurricane Melissa, SLC was honoured to partner with The House of God Church, Bog Walk; Y&C Tours; and Upliftment Early Childhood Learning Centre from Portmore in a humanitarian effort to support families in Cane Cunnan, Westmoreland.

Through the generosity and dedication of our partners, donors, and volunteers, we were able to provide assistance to over 50 children and families affected by the hurricane.

Beyond the assistance provided, the initiative represented a message of hope, care, and solidarity for those affected. SLC Foundation continues to believe in serving with compassion, protecting the dignity of those we assist, and creating meaningful impact through partnership and service.

50+ Children & Families Supported
Meaningful Assistance

Support delivered through partnership.

The response was made possible through the combined generosity, dedication, and service of partners, donors, and volunteers working together to assist those affected by Hurricane Melissa.

Dignity First

Why there is no recipient photo gallery.

Some moments are meant to be served, not documented.

In keeping with our commitment to dignity and respect, the SLC Foundation chose not to record photographs or videos of the families and children supported through this initiative.

This decision was made to protect their privacy and preserve their personal experiences during a difficult period in their lives.

For us, meaningful service does not require a camera. The priority was ensuring that assistance reached the people who needed it while treating every individual with care and respect.
