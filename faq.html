Admissions & Registration

Regular registration may close once available spaces have been filled, but pre-registration may remain available for prospective students. Families can submit an online application to express interest in placement and begin the admissions process.
No. Submission of an application does not guarantee admission. Placement is subject to availability, review of the information provided, required documentation, and completion of the registration process.
Requirements vary by division, but may include:
  • Child's Birth Certificate
  • Immunization Record
  • Passport-sized photographs
  • Parent or Guardian Identification
  • Previous School Report, where applicable
  • Additional school records for Preparatory applicants
02

Programmes & Placement

Our Early Childhood division serves children from Nursery through Pre-Kindergarten, while our Kindergarten & Preparatory division serves Kindergarten through Grade 6.
Placement is generally based on age, current grade level, previous school history, and developmental readiness. If you are unsure, you can request a call and a member of our team can help guide you.
You may indicate the prospective grade on the application. Final placement is determined after the school reviews the student's information and any relevant academic records.

School Life

Yes. Activities may include Music & Dance, Swimming, Karate, Spelling Bee, Speech & Drama, Football, Spanish, Brownies, and 4H Club.

04

Fees & Payments

Yes. Registration fees vary by division. The applicable amount and payment instructions are provided during the registration process.
Fee information can be found on the relevant Early Childhood or Preparatory fees page of the website.
Regardless of when your child is enrolled during the school year, the M.I.P fee must still be paid. For example, if your child is enrolled in January, the fee is payable upon registration and again in September if they continue attending school.
No. The M.I.P Protection Package must be paid in full and cannot be separated into individual components.
In exceptional circumstances such as a critical illness resulting in ten (10) or more consecutive excused absences, tuition for the missed days will be credited to the next billing period.

A medical report or physician's sick leave certificate is required. Credits apply only when tuition for the current period has been paid in full.
Different payment methods may attract different charges.
  • Cash payments at school incur no additional fee.
  • Bank deposits are subject to your bank's charges.
  • Online bank transfers may attract ACH or RTGS fees depending on your financial institution.
  • Website online checkout may include applicable GCT.
  • Payments made through the school's POS terminal include a service fee.
