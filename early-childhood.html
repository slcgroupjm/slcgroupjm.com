Our Story

Born from a need. Built with purpose.

Sunshine Learning Center was birthed during the onset of the COVID-19 pandemic, when our Principal and founder recognised the need for a learning facility and internet centre that could provide both care and meaningful support for young children.

The Center officially began on September 7, 2020, responding to families searching for an institution that understood their children's needs and developmental stages.

Today, that same commitment continues through a learning environment focused on care, development, readiness, confidence, and strong early foundations.

Our Mission

A safe place to explore, discover and learn.

Our mission is to create a safe, nurturing, and stimulating environment where children from 6 months to 6 years are encouraged to explore, discover, and learn. Through meaningful experiences and developmentally appropriate activities, we promote readiness in literacy and numeracy while supporting each child's physical, mental, and emotional growth.

02
Our Vision

Quality education. Strong leadership. Lasting impact.

Our vision is to deliver high-quality education at a reasonable cost while building a financially stable and self-sufficient institution. We are committed to empowering and developing our staff and students as we work toward becoming the school of choice within our cluster, parish, and across the country.
